Track multiple drones without losing sight of the mission.

I designed the fleet-map experience for operating multiple drones across large areas with docking stations. The work connected live tracking, route tracers, AI-synthesized incidents, and a panel that brings the drone’s camera view, mechanical status, and mission status onto the map.

Cover a large area with a coordinated fleet

The design problem was how to operate multiple drones across a large area while keeping track of each flight. Docking stations were part of that coverage model. The fleet map needed to show where drones were, how they were moving, and which incidents needed attention during a live mission.

Live tracking

See where each drone is during the mission.

Fleet coverage

Coordinate multiple drones and docking stations across a large area.

Incident context

Connect AI-synthesized incident information to its location on the route.

Make incidents understandable on the map

An incident marker needs to tell more than where something happened. I connected the marker to the drone’s route and live panel, so operators could review the AI-synthesized summary without losing track of the flight.

Keep the route, camera, and live status together

The map panel was designed to work as the drone’s viewfinder while also showing mechanical and mission status. Keeping those views together lets the operator review an incident in the context of the flight, rather than treating the video and map as separate experiences.

What I owned

  • The fleet-map tracking experience for multiple drones and docking stations covering large areas.
  • The visual treatment of route tracers and incident indicators on the fleet map.
  • The interaction design for AI-synthesized incidents and a live map panel combining the camera view, mechanical status, and mission status.

01

Check the equipment

The inventory view lists drones, docks, and controllers, with device status and alerts available before an operator opens a live feed.

02

Follow the fleet on the map

I designed the route tracer to show a drone’s path on the fleet map, along with the visual indicators for incidents. These details make the selected flight readable within a map that also needs to support multiple drones.

03

A live viewfinder with mission and mechanical status

I designed the map panel to serve as the drone’s live viewfinder and show mechanical and mission status. The operator can keep the camera, the flight route, and the drone’s status in view during a live situation.
