Accessibility

A commitment we keep working on.

We consider accessibility throughout design and development, using WCAG 2.2 Level AA as our target and the Revised Section 508 Standards where applicable.

Last updated November 15, 2026

Our target

Our target for this website is WCAG 2.2 Level AA. It guides our decisions about content, controls, navigation, and interaction. This is a design and development commitment, not a claim of verified conformance.

The Revised Section 508 Standards inform work where those requirements apply. They incorporate WCAG 2.0 Level A and AA for covered content and include requirements beyond web content. Referencing them here does not establish that this portfolio is Section 508 compliant.

W3C: WCAG 2.2 · U.S. Access Board: Revised Section 508 Standards

Where evaluation stands

The implementation includes labeled controls, visible keyboard-focus styles, and reduced-motion support. Development checks cover selected guide interactions, including panel state, navigation, and conversation continuity. Those checks do not amount to an accessibility audit.

We have not completed a full WCAG conformance evaluation of the site. Keyboard operation, screen-reader use, contrast in both themes, zoom and reflow, and complete Oslo and Eda workflows still need systematic evaluation across pages and interaction states.

Current limitations

Some case-study screenshots contain text, charts, or interface details that are not fully reproduced in the surrounding descriptions. Nearby explanations provide context, but may not communicate every detail in the original image.

Oslo and Eda can explain the work and accessibility considerations. Their replies are not automated accessibility tests, audit results, or confirmation that an interface meets a standard.

This is not an exhaustive list of barriers. We will update this statement as issues are identified and evaluations are completed.

Tell us about a barrier

If something makes this site difficult to use, email uxterrain@gmail.com. Include the page URL, what you were trying to do, and what got in the way. If useful, you can also share your browser, device, or assistive technology. Share only what you are comfortable providing.

You can also ask for clarification or a text explanation of case-study material.
