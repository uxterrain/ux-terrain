Your questions, your choice.

Oslo and Eda help you explore the portfolio. If a question would make the work clearer, you can choose to share it privately with Jimmy.

What sharing saves

Only the question you review and submit, the guide, the case study or page, the walkthrough station when relevant, a submission reference, and the submission time are saved in a private question inbox. This feature does not collect your name or email address, save your full conversation, or attach an advertising identifier or IP address to the record. Leave personal and confidential information out of the question itself.

How the questions are used

Jimmy can review questions and recurring topics to improve the explanations and project knowledge. Visitor questions are suggestions, not verified facts. They are not automatically added to the agents’ source material. The question inbox has no public reading endpoint.

Sharing is optional

Opening the sharing form does not save a question. Selecting “Share question” sends the text shown in that form. You can edit it first. Sharing a question does not request a personal reply.

Asking Jimmy directly

“Ask Jimmy about this” opens your email application with a draft containing your question and the case-study link. Review the draft and choose whether to send it. The website does not send an email for you.

Conversations with the guides

Your messages are sent to the site’s AI service to generate replies. The site keeps limited conversation continuity in your browser tab so you can follow references and continue a discussion. The private question inbox is a separate, optional feature; ordinary guide conversations are not copied into it automatically.

Questions or deletion requests

Email uxterrain@gmail.com if you have a question about this feature or want a shared question removed. Include the question and approximate submission date so it can be found. Do not include additional sensitive details.
